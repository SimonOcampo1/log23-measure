The two-base development adapts the separated-weight interpolation and
Taylor-determinant framework of OpenAI's "The irrationality exponent of
pi is 2" and relies on substantial OpenAI formal infrastructure.
The new ratio-specific modules retain their existing source comments.
The dependency-closure verifier adapts the earlier VerifyGeneralLog
verifier, as stated in its source.

Bundled third-party formal library:
  OpenAI math, revision adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a
  https://github.com/openai/math
  Retained licenses:
    vendor/openai-math/LICENSE
    vendor/openai-math/lean/LICENSE

The vendored sources are copied byte-for-byte from the verified local
snapshot. SOURCE-INVENTORY.json records their original paths and hashes.
The publication package does not alter upstream proof modules.

External dependency:
