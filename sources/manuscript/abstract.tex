We prove that the irrationality exponent of
\(\theta=\log_2 3\) is at most three. More precisely, for every
\(\varepsilon>0\), every sufficiently large positive integer \(q\), and
every integer \(p\), one has
\(\lvert\theta-p/q\rvert\geq q^{-3-\varepsilon}\).
The proof combines weighted interpolation at the points
\((2^j,3^j)\) with denominator clearing and a joint count of auxiliary
and analytic Taylor indices. A curve inequality establishes the required
interpolation, and a choice of rational parameters yields the bound.
The proof is formalized in Lean.
